Lignarc Dashboard – Privacy Notice

Operated by VHG Living Furniture Joint Stock Company (brand: Lignarc), 22/166B Alley 210, Street 11, Thu Duc Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Contact: support@vhg.vn.

Purpose

The Lignarc Dashboard is an internal reporting tool used only by Lignarc's own staff. It connects to our own Amazon seller and advertising accounts (self-authorized) to show our sales, inventory and advertising performance.

Data we access

Aggregated business data from our own accounts: orders, units, revenue, fees, inventory levels, and advertising campaign metrics (impressions, clicks, spend, sales). We do not request, collect or store buyer personal information such as names, addresses, email addresses or phone numbers.

Storage and security

Data is retrieved over HTTPS, stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM), and can be viewed only by authorized Lignarc staff with a password. API credentials are kept in an encrypted secrets store and are never published.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with any third party.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for internal reporting. Authorization can be revoked at any time from Amazon Seller Central or Amazon Ads, after which no new data is retrieved.
